What we do

Three public lines of work, each with a clear job: teach a skill, answer the phone for a trade, or build a place to watch programmed television.

Training

Digital Business Academy

Independent, hands-on skills courses for builders, working professionals, and career-changers. Self-paced lessons and applied work, sold by Digital Plug Co. Certificates of completion only; the academy is not accredited and does not promise a job or income outcome.

Visit Digital Business Academy

Trade services

Septic answering for central Ohio

An AI plus human answering and booking service for septic pumping, installation, and inspection contractors in Knox, Hocking, Madison, and Licking counties. We answer the phone in the contractor's company name, take intake, and help schedule the work.

Visit the septic answering page

Who we are

Digital Plug Co. is a software company based in Columbus, Ohio. Our mission is to make the underserved visible: trades that miss calls after hours, learners who need practical skills rather than a degree program, and audiences who want programmed nostalgia television with rights cleared in the open.

We keep the public story tied to what is actually running. If a product is still being built, the page says so. If a legal or licensing question is still open, we do not paper over it.
